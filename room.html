    FAQ Answers to common questions, plus quick card references.
    01 Getting started
    How do I join a game? Select Join to take an open seat. Your hand and controls appear at the bottom.
    What can I do before the round starts? Before the round, use + to add cards, select a card to flip it, or drag it to the discard pile.
    How does a round start? When at least two players are seated, select Play. Every hand refreshes with seven cards.
    How do I arrange my hand? Use Sort to arrange your cards. The selected order is saved when you move.
    02 Playing and winning
    Which card can I play? Match the top discard by suit or value, or play a wild card. Drag your card to the discard pile.
    Can I play after drawing? Draw your allowed cards. You may still play afterward; otherwise select Pass.
    When does a round end? Play your final card or the seven of hearts to end the round.
    Who wins? The player with the lowest remaining hand penalty wins.
    03 Special cards
    Eight Skips the next player.
    Jack Reverses direction with three or more players.
    Ace Choose the next suit. The ace of spades is the exception.
    Two Stacks or adds a two-card draw penalty.
    Seven of hearts Ends the game immediately and triggers scoring.
    Joker Stacks or adds a four-card draw penalty.
    Ace of spades Plays on any card and blocks a draw penalty.
    04 How is my penalty calculated?

    Your penalty is the sum of the ranks of the cards left in your hand when the round ends. For example, a two (20) and a king (13) add up to 33 penalty. The lowest penalty wins.
