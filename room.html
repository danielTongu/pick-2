Room guide Learn the game in a minute, then open any section when you need a reminder.
01Quick start
Open a gameCreate a Local game or view an available Network game.
Play the gameSelect Play. Everyone receives seven cards.
Take your turnDrag a card to the discard pile, draw when needed, or pass after drawing.
Use your cardsSelect a card to flip it. Sort your hand locally until you move.
02Core rules
Match the discardPlay the same suit or value as the top discard. Wild cards play at any time.
Draw and passDraw your allowed cards. You may still play afterward; otherwise select Pass.
Finish the gamePlay your final card, or the seven of hearts, to end the game.
Lowest score winsThe player with the lowest remaining hand score wins.
03Special cards
EightSkips the next player.
JackReverses direction with three or more players.
AceChoose the next suit. The ace of spades is the exception.
TwoStacks or adds a two-card draw penalty.
JokerStacks or adds a four-card draw penalty.
Ace of spadesPlays on any card and blocks a draw penalty.
Seven of heartsEnds the game immediately and triggers scoring.
