FAQ Match setup, rules, special cards, and scoring.
01 Getting started
How do I take a seat? Before a match starts, select Join to take an open seat. Your hand and controls appear below the table.
What can I do before the match starts? Practice with the controls: use + to draw a card, select a card to flip it, or drag it to the discard pile. A new match replaces your practice hand.
How do I start a match? Once at least two players are seated, select Start and choose One match or Knockout. Each player receives seven cards. The deck contains 52 standard cards and two jokers.
What is a penalty? A hand penalty is the total points for cards left in your hand at the end of a match. Lower is better. A draw penalty requires you to draw two or four cards unless you play a defending card.
02 Playing and winning
Which card can I play? Match the top discard’s suit or value, or play a wild card: a joker or ace of spades. Drag your card to the discard area.
What happens after a suit is chosen? Play the chosen suit, any ace, or a joker. If the top discard is wild and no suit or draw penalty is active, you may play any card.
How do I draw or pass? Use + to draw one card, or the full amount of a draw penalty. After drawing one card, you may still play. Select Pass to end your turn and draw any cards still owed.
How do I defend a draw penalty? Defend a two with a two, joker, or ace of spades; defend a joker with a joker or ace of spades. A defending two passes two cards to the next player; a joker passes four. Ace of spades cancels the penalty. Penalties do not add up. Drawing the penalty ends your turn.
How do I arrange my hand? Use Sort to order your cards by rank or suit, or select none.
When does a match end? Legally playing your final card or the seven of hearts ends the match immediately. No further card effect is applied.
Who wins one match? The player with the lowest hand penalty wins. Players tied for the lowest total share the win. An empty hand scores zero.
How does Knockout work? Knockout is a series of matches. In matches with three or more players, everyone tied for the highest hand penalty is eliminated. If only one player remains, they win; if none remain, the series is tied. The final two play one match: the lowest hand penalty wins, and equal totals tie.
When does the next Knockout match start? If at least two players qualify, the next match starts after 30 seconds. A qualified human player can select Start to begin sooner. Each match deals fresh seven-card hands.
03 Special cards
Eight Skips the next player’s turn.
Jack Reverses turn order with three or more players. With two players, skips the other player’s turn.
Ace of clubs, diamonds, or hearts Lets you choose the suit the next player must follow. Must be legally played first.
Two Gives the next player a two-card draw penalty unless they defend.
Seven of hearts Ends the match immediately and triggers scoring. Must be legally played first.
Joker Wild. Plays on any card and gives the next player a four-card draw penalty unless they defend.
Ace of spades Wild. Plays on any card and cancels a draw penalty. Does not let you choose a suit.
