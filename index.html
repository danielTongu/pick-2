Game guide Learn the table in a minute, then open any section when you need a reminder.
01Quick start
Take your seat

Enter your name and join the three AI opponents.

Start the game

Select Start. Everyone receives seven cards.

Take your turn

Drag a card to the discard pile, draw when needed, or pass after drawing.

Use your cards

Select a card to flip it. Sort your hand locally until you move.

02Core rules
Match the discard

Play the same suit or value as the top discard. Wild cards play at any time.

Draw and pass

Draw your allowed cards. You may still play afterward; otherwise select Pass.

Finish the game

Play your final card, or the seven of hearts, to end the game.

Lowest score wins

The player with the lowest remaining hand score wins.

03Special cards
EightSkips the next player.
JackReverses direction with three or more players.
AceChoose the next suit. The ace of spades is the exception.
TwoStacks or adds a two-card draw penalty.
JokerStacks or adds a four-card draw penalty.
Ace of spadesPlays on any card and blocks a draw penalty.
Seven of heartsEnds the game immediately and triggers scoring.
