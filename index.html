FAQ About the game and choosing a room.
01 About Pick 2
What is Pick 2? A card game of matching suits and values, using special cards, and trying to empty your hand.
What do I need to play? Just your browser. Play free, with no download or account required.
What does it mean to be an Actor? An Actor is a player who takes a seat, holds a hand of cards, and makes moves on their turn.
What does it mean to be a Viewer? A Viewer is a spectator who follows the game without a seat, a hand, or a turn. Before a match starts, a Viewer can select Join in the room to become an Actor if a seat is available.
02 Choosing a room
What is the difference between Direct and Hosted? Direct runs rooms in your browser. Hosted connects you to shared rooms where you can meet other players.
Should I create or join a room? Create opens a new room with your chosen number of seats. Join takes an available seat in the room you name. Your actor name identifies you in the room.
Can I watch without playing? Yes. Select a Live Rooms row to enter as a viewer without taking a seat. The list shows room status and seat counts; use the status filter to narrow it down.
Where can I learn the rules? The room’s FAQ explains how to take a seat, start a match, play your cards, and understand the results.
