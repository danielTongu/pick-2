About Pick 2 & how to play

Pick 2 is a card game where you match suits or values, use special cards, and try to empty your hand. Play free in your browser, with no download or account required.

Each player starts with seven cards from a standard deck plus two jokers.

Start a match

  1. Enter your name and a room name below, then choose Create or Join.
  2. Select Enter room.
  3. Select Play in the room: No for one match, Yes for Knockout.

Take your turn

  • Play: drag a card matching the top discard’s suit or value. Jokers and the ace of spades are wild.
  • Draw: use + to draw one card. You can still play afterward.
  • Pass: end your turn. Any remaining draw allowance is drawn automatically.

After a suit is declared, play that suit, any ace, or a joker. Any card can follow a wild when no suit or penalty is active.

Special cards

Two
The next player draws two, unless they defend.
Joker
Wild. The next player draws four, unless they defend.
Eight
Skips the next player.
Jack
Reverses direction; skips the other player in a two-player match.
Ace of clubs, diamonds or hearts
Once legally played, lets you choose the next suit.
Ace of spades
Wild. Cancels a draw penalty without choosing a suit.
Seven of hearts
Once legally played, ends the match and triggers scoring.

Draw penalties: defend a two with a two, joker, or ace of spades; defend a joker with a joker or ace of spades. A defensive two passes a two-card penalty; a joker passes four. Penalties do not accumulate. Drawing the penalty ends your turn.

Scoring & winning

Your final card or a legal seven of hearts ends the match, before any further card effect. Add the ranks left in each hand: lowest total wins; equal lowest totals tie.

Knockout

Play a series with fresh hands each match.

  • Three or more players: everyone tied for the highest penalty is eliminated.
  • Next match: starts after 30 seconds, or sooner when a qualified human selects Play.
  • Final two: lowest penalty wins; a tie stays a tie.

One qualifier wins the series. No qualifiers means a tie.
