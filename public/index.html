Game guide Learn the table in a minute, then open any section when you need a reminder.
01 Quick start
Enter a room

Watch as a visitor or join as a player. Your hand appears below the discard pile.

Start the game

With at least two players present, select Start. Everyone receives seven cards.

Take your turn

Drag a card from your hand to the discard pile, draw when needed, or pass after drawing.

Use your cards

Select a card to flip it. Use the sort control to arrange your hand locally until you move.

02 Core rules
Match the discard

Play the same suit or value as the top discard. Wild cards can be played at any time.

Draw and pass

Draw your allowed cards when needed. You may still play afterward; otherwise, select Pass.

Finish the game

Play your final card, or play the seven of hearts to end the game immediately.

Lowest score wins

When the game ends, the player with the lowest remaining hand score wins.

03 Special cards
Eight Skips the next player.
Jack Skips in a two-player game; reverses direction with three or more.
Ace Choose the suit that continues play. The ace of spades is the exception.
Two The next player must stack a two or joker, or block with the ace of spades, or draw two and lose the turn.
Joker The next player must stack a joker, or block with the ace of spades, or draw four and lose the turn.
Ace of spades Plays on any card and blocks an active draw penalty.
Seven of hearts Ends the game immediately and triggers scoring.
